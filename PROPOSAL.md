## 🚀 The Startup Idea

**NightProof** is a privacy-preserving verification platform built on the Midnight Network that enables citizens to prove eligibility for government schemes, scholarships, subsidies, and welfare programs *without* revealing their personal documents or sensitive information.

Today, applicants repeatedly upload Aadhaar copies, income certificates, caste certificates, disability records, and academic documents across multiple portals. This creates unnecessary privacy risks, duplicate verification costs, and massive document leaks. 

NightProof replaces document sharing with **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs)**. It allows users to prove only the required condition—such as family income below a threshold, age above 18, or scholarship eligibility—while the underlying data remains completely private and off-chain.

Instead of exposing sensitive records, encrypted credentials generate cryptographic proofs that Midnight smart contracts verify privately on-chain. Institutions receive only a verified **yes/no result** with an auditable proof, giving citizens full ownership of their data through selective disclosure.

---

## 🌍 Real Use Case

A student applying for a government scholarship verifies their credentials once through an authorized issuer. 

When applying for the scholarship, NightProof generates a private eligibility proof confirming that the student satisfies all required criteria **without revealing their income, identity details, or supporting documents.** 

The scholarship portal instantly verifies the proof, reducing processing time from weeks to seconds while completely protecting the student's personal privacy. The same infrastructure can later support pensions, farmer subsidies, healthcare assistance, and other public welfare programs seamlessly.

## ⚡ Why Midnight Network?

- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs:** Verify eligibility without exposing personal data.
- **Private Smart Contracts:** Keep sensitive information confidential on-chain.
- **On-Chain Verification:** Creates tamper-proof and auditable trust between citizens and institutions.
- **Selective Disclosure:** Ensures users reveal *only* what regulations require, and nothing more.

---

## 🎯 Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

NightProof delivers a complete end-to-end verification workflow where citizens:
1. Securely store encrypted digital credentials.
2. Generate eligibility proofs for income, age, and academic criteria.
3. Submit them to institutions through Midnight Compact smart contracts. 

The verification portal displays only the proof outcome instead of sensitive documents, demonstrating exactly how privacy-preserving verification can replace traditional document-based approval systems.
