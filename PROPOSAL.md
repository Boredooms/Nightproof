# NightProof — Privacy-First Eligibility Verification Platform

## Startup Idea

NightProof is a privacy-preserving verification platform built on Midnight Network that enables citizens to prove eligibility for government schemes, scholarships, subsidies, and welfare programs without revealing their personal documents or sensitive information.

Today, applicants repeatedly upload Aadhaar copies, income certificates, caste certificates, disability records, and academic documents across multiple portals, creating unnecessary privacy risks, duplicate verification costs, and document leaks. NightProof replaces document sharing with Zero-Knowledge Proofs, allowing users to prove only the required condition—such as family income below a threshold, age above 18, or scholarship eligibility—while the underlying data remains completely private.

Instead of exposing sensitive records, encrypted credentials generate cryptographic proofs that Midnight smart contracts verify privately on-chain. Institutions receive only a verified yes/no result with an auditable proof, giving citizens full ownership of their data through selective disclosure.

## Real Use Case

A student applying for a government scholarship verifies their credentials once through an authorized issuer. When applying, NightProof generates a private eligibility proof confirming that the student satisfies all required criteria without revealing income, identity details, or supporting documents. The scholarship portal instantly verifies the proof, reducing processing time while protecting personal privacy. The same infrastructure can later support pensions, farmer subsidies, healthcare assistance, and other public welfare programs.

## Why Midnight Network

- Zero-Knowledge Proofs verify eligibility without exposing personal data.
- Private smart contracts keep sensitive information confidential.
- On-chain verification creates tamper-proof and auditable trust.
- Selective disclosure ensures users reveal only what regulations require.

## MVP

NightProof will deliver a complete end-to-end verification workflow where citizens securely store encrypted digital credentials, generate eligibility proofs for income, age, and academic criteria, and submit them to institutions through Midnight Compact smart contracts. The verification portal displays only the proof outcome instead of sensitive documents, demonstrating how privacy-preserving verification can replace traditional document-based approval systems.

## Vision

NightProof aims to become the universal privacy layer for digital public services, where one verified credential can securely unlock scholarships, subsidies, pensions, healthcare assistance, and future governance systems without forcing citizens to repeatedly expose their personal information.
